Maintenance Technician
About Haldane
Haldane Group makes packaging, food ingredients and machine parts in 6 plants across Belgium.
Our plants run on SAP.
Your role
You find the cause of faults in wiring, motors and control cabinets.
You will work daily with PLC programming and keep TIA Portal projects up to date.
You plan inspections and part replacements before machines break down.
Your profile
No prior experience with welding is required.
You can read electrical schematics.
You speak fluent Dutch; French is a plus.
Nice to have
Experience with hydraulics is a plus.
Knowledge of root cause analysis would be nice.
What we offer
A permanent contract in a stable company.
Training in lean manufacturing.
