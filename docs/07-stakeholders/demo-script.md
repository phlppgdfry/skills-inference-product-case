# Demo script (8 minutes)

For a mixed audience: HR partners, a plant manager, the PM. Uses the [browser demo](../../site/demo/index.html); nothing is sent anywhere.

1. **The problem, in their words (1 min).** "Writing a profile takes 45 minutes and two people never write the same one." Show one generated job description with company boilerplate.
2. **Paste and draft (1 min).** Pick *Maintenance technician*. Point at the highlights: every skill has its sentence. Hover a semantic match: "it understood 'you find the cause of faults in wiring' as electrical troubleshooting".
3. **What it does not do (1.5 min).** Point at "Our plants run on SAP": not suggested. "No experience with welding needed": not suggested. "Training in lean": not suggested. This is where trust is won.
4. **Please check (1 min).** Show an item in the review band. "It asks only when it has a real chance of being right."
5. **Fairness (1.5 min).** Type "He will report to the plant manager" and "Join our young and dynamic team" into the text. The profile does not move; the wording is flagged with a suggestion. Tell the story of the first eval run (60% invariance) and the fix.
6. **Dutch (30 s).** Switch to the Dutch sample. Same quality.
7. **Honest limits (1 min).** "These are test texts. Week one of the pilot runs 40 of your ads before anyone relies on it." Show the evals page and the EVIDENCE file.
8. **The ask (30 s).** Two HR partners, four weeks, 20 profiles each.

## Likely questions
- *"Can it do our French ads?"* Not yet; Dutch and English aliases first, French is next in the taxonomy plan.
- *"Will it rank our people?"* That is a separate decision with different legal duties; not in this pilot.
- *"What if it is wrong?"* You reject it with one click; that verdict is the training data for the next version.
